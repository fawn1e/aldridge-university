Admissions

We do not seek students. Students find us.

Aldridge University is not an institution one simply applies to. Admission is a privilege extended to those who belong — by blood, by bond, or by exceptional circumstance.

Pathways to Admission

I

Legacy Admission

Children and direct relatives of the founding families — Winthrop, St. John, and Harcourt — receive automatic admission. No application required. No questions asked.

Guaranteed
II

Referral Admission

Those with connections to alumni, Board members, or 'friends of the institution' may be considered. A personal recommendation from the right person opens doors that remain closed to others.

By Invitation
III

Merit Admission

Rarely, Aldridge opens limited positions for exceptional candidates. These individuals must demonstrate not only academic excellence but... potential usefulness to the institution's interests.

Highly Selective

Requirements

Standardized Testing (USA)

Required for Merit Admission candidates

SAT Minimum 1500 / 1600
ACT Minimum 34 / 36
GPA Minimum 3.9 / 4.0 (unweighted)
AP Courses Minimum 5 courses, scores 4-5

The Aldridge Examinations

Required for all non-Legacy candidates

The Aldridge Assessment A comprehensive evaluation of critical thinking, ethical flexibility, and decision-making under pressure.
The Lineage Review Background verification extending three generations. Family history, associations, and... discretions are examined.
The Interview A personal meeting with representatives of the Board. Location and format vary. Preparation is... inadvisable.
The Discretion Evaluation The nature of this examination is not disclosed to candidates beforehand. Those who pass understand why. Those who fail... do not remember.

A Note to Prospective Students

If you are reading this and believe you qualify for Merit Admission, you likely do not. Those who belong at Aldridge do not search for us. They are found.
